# AI-GAs (Clune 2019) and The AI Scientist (Lu et al. 2024)

- **Authors:** Jeff Clune (AI-GAs); Chris Lu, Cong Lu, Robert Tjarko Lange, Jakob Foerster, Jeff Clune, David Ha (AI Scientist)
- **Year:** 2019 / 2024
- **Venue:** arXiv 1905.10985 / arXiv 2408.06292 (later versions and AI Scientist-v2 exist; venue details **[unverified]**)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.10985 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06292

## TL;DR
AI-GAs: instead of hand-assembling AGI, build algorithms that *generate* AI via three pillars — meta-learn architectures, meta-learn learning algorithms, auto-generate environments. The AI Scientist automates the research loop (idea → code → experiments → paper → automated review), a concrete instance of AI generating AI-research artefacts.

## Key results
AI Scientist produced full ML papers at ~US$15 each with an automated reviewer near human-level agreement **[from memory, unverified]**.

## Why it matters for RSI
The research program that connects open-endedness to automated AI R&D — the strongest form of RSI.

## Limitations
Weng (2026): paper production ≠ discovery — fabricated citations, implementation drift, weak experiments.
